FAQ

Common questions, answered honestly.

What exactly do I get for ₹499?

A professionally designed one-page website with your business name, description, contact details, a WhatsApp contact button, basic on-page SEO setup, social media links, and one revision round. Deployment assistance is included. Domain and hosting are arranged separately.

Is the domain included?

No. Domain registration is a separate third-party cost, typically ₹700–1,200 per year depending on the extension. We can help you connect an existing domain or guide you to register a new one.

Is hosting included?

No. Hosting is a separate third-party cost. We can recommend affordable options and assist with deployment. Many hosts offer free tiers suitable for a one-page website.

Will the website work on mobile?

Yes. Every website we build is mobile-first and tested on common phone, tablet, and desktop screen sizes.

Can I connect WhatsApp?

Yes. The starter package includes a WhatsApp contact button that opens a chat with your business number.

How long does delivery take?

Typical delivery is 3–5 working days after we receive your business information and confirm the scope. Complex requests may take longer.

Can I request changes?

The starter package includes one round of revisions. Additional changes can be discussed and quoted separately.

Who owns the completed website?

You own the website content and the final deliverables once the project is complete and paid for. Third-party assets (if any) remain subject to their own licenses.

Is ongoing maintenance included?

No. Ongoing maintenance is not included in the ₹499 package. It can be arranged separately if you need content updates or technical support after launch.

Can I request additional pages or features?

Yes. Multi-page websites, booking systems, e-commerce, and custom features can be quoted separately based on your requirements. We'll give you a clear price before starting any additional work.
