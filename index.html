FAQ

Your questions, answered.

What exactly do I get for ₹499?

A professionally designed one-page website — with your business name, description, contact details, WhatsApp button, basic on-page SEO, social media links, and 1 revision round. Deployment assistance is included. Domain and hosting are arranged separately.

Is ₹499 a limited-time offer?

Yes. The regular price is ₹999. The ₹499 offer is available for a limited period — and for a limited number of new clients each month. Offer valid till 31 Dec 2026.

Are domain and hosting included?

No. Domain registration is a third-party cost of ₹700–1,200/year. Hosting is also separate. We recommend the right options and help with deployment — at no extra charge.

Do I need to pay an advance?

No. You review the website first, and pay only when you're satisfied. This keeps your risk at zero.

What if I don't like the design?

Starter includes 1 revision round, Growth includes 2, and Pro includes 3. If you're still not satisfied, there's no payment — we simply hand over the source files to you.

Will the website work on mobile?

Absolutely. Every website is mobile-first. 79% of Indian customers come via mobile, so we prioritise perfect mobile display — tested on phone, tablet, and desktop.

How does WhatsApp integration work?

Your website has a WhatsApp button. When a customer clicks it, a chat opens directly on your business number — with a pre-filled message. It's the most effective conversion tool in India.

How long does delivery take?

Starter and Growth: 3 working days after we receive your content. Pro: 5–7 working days. Complex custom requests may take longer.

Will my website rank on Google?

We set up basic on-page SEO (title, meta description, headings, schema) — which helps Google understand your website. But honestly: top ranking requires regular content, backlinks, and time. We don't promise guaranteed #1 rankings — no honest developer does.

Which cities do you serve?

All of India — remotely. Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Lucknow — any city. Everything is handled over WhatsApp and email.

How do I make the payment?

UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm) or bank transfer. Payment is made only after you approve the website — no advance. We provide an invoice.
